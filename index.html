ABOUT ROYAL STORES

Expert Fabric Vulcanizing & Garment Repair

રોયલ સ્ટોર્સ - વર્ષોથી ફાટેલા કપડાં અને બેગના વલ્કેનાઇઝિંગ તેમજ રીપેરીંગનું વિશ્વસનીય નામ.

Royal Stores provides specialized clothing repair services tailored for everyday and premium apparel. Whether it is an accidental tear on your favorite denim, a split seam on a formal shirt, a worn jacket, or a damaged travel bag, our team repairs and reinforces garments using modern fabric vulcanizing technology.

What is Fabric Vulcanizing? (વલ્કેનાઇઝિંગ શું છે?)

Fabric vulcanizing is an advanced heat-press bonding method using specialized adhesive film (as seen on our visiting card). It fuses fabric fibers securely without leaving bulky, irritating stitches, giving your clothes long-lasting strength through daily washes.
